About Me

I’m Moazzem Hossen, Founder of MH Creactionx, a Publicity Designer with 2 years of professional experience and 4 years of dedicated practice.

I’ve worked on both local and international (Bangladeshi & overseas) movie and natok projects, specializing in movie posters, natok & web series posters, and music video publicity. My design approach focuses on cinematic composition, color grading, typography, and realistic retouching to deliver high-impact visuals.

I approach every project with a strong sense of storytelling, branding, and audience psychology, ensuring premium results that stand out across digital platforms.
