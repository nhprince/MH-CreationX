# Sales & Outreach Strategy: Custom Studio Development

Instead of selling a fixed product, your strategy is to offer **Custom Development Services** using StudioFlow as a powerful, working foundation. You are selling the *result* (a perfectly tailored system), not just the software.

## The Pitch: "Your Agency, Your Rules"
When outreaching, emphasize that you don't believe in "one size fits all." Use StudioFlow to show what is possible, then offer to build a version specifically for their unique workflow.

## Target Audience
- **Boutique Agencies**: Looking for a specialized tool that the big "Generic" PM tools (like Monday.com or Asana) can't provide.
- **Growing Production Houses**: Who have outgrown their spreadsheets and need a dedicated system.
- **Enterprise Clients**: Who need specific security (Audit Trails) and private hosting.

## Outreach Messaging (The Hooks)
1. **"Built for You, Not the Masses"**: Show them StudioFlow and say: *"This is a system I developed for a similar agency. I can customize every single button, field, and calculation to fit YOUR studio exactly."*
2. **"Owned, Not Rented"**: Unlike SaaS, you are offering to develop and deploy a system that they *own*. This is a huge selling point for privacy-conscious clients.
3. **"Direct Developer Access"**: They aren't talking to a support bot; they are talking to the developer (NH Prince) who will build their vision.

## The Sales Process
1. **The Demo (The "Aha" Moment)**: Show the existing StudioFlow features to prove your capability.
2. **The Discovery**: Ask: *"What is the one thing in your current workflow that frustrates you?"*
3. **The Customization Offer**: Map their frustration to a feature change in StudioFlow. (e.g., *"Oh, you need to track 'Social Media Influencer' classes? I can easily add that to the Registry for you."*)
4. **The Deployment**: Charge for the customization and the initial setup/deployment.

## Pricing (Service Model)
- **Base Setup Fee**: Deployment and training on the standard StudioFlow system.
- **Customization Modules**: Tiered pricing based on how many new features they need.
- **Maintenance/Support**: Monthly retainer for updates and hosting support.
