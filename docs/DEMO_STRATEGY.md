# Demo Execution: Characterizing the "Possibilities"

A demo of StudioFlow is your chance to show a client their *future* system. Focus on showing that while the system is powerful out of the box, it is infinitely flexible for their needs.

## 1. Showcase the "Base" Power
Start by showing the standard Dashboard. Explain that this is the "Foundation" you will build upon for them. 
- Show how fast the filters work.
- Show how the Analytics reflect real-time data.

## 2. Talk about "The Customization"
During the demo, don't just say "here is the feature." Say: *"This is how the system currently looks, but for your studio, we can change these categories or add a special field for [Client Goal]."*

## 3. The "Wow" Flow (Sequence)
1. **The Entrance**: Open the Dashboard and focus on the financial clarity (Revenue Cleared).
2. **The Flexibility**: Go to the Client Registry. Show how you can add a "Custom Partner Class" on the fly. This proves the system isn't "locked."
3. **The Professionalism**: Generate a **Cash Memo**. Tell them: *"We will customize this template to match your exact branding and letterhead."*
4. **The Client Portal**: Show the OTP login. Explain that this gives their clients a "Premium" experience that competitors don't offer.

## 4. Discovery Questions to Ask During Demo
- *"Would you need to track any specific information for your clients that you don't see here?"*
- *"How do you currently handle payments? We can customize the logic to match your specific workflow."*
- *"Which service categories drive the most revenue for you? We can highlight those in the Analytics."*

## 5. Impression Checklist
- [ ] Use Dark Mode (it feels 'Premium' and 'Custom').
- [ ] Have a few custom-named projects that match their specific niche (e.g., "Cinema Production" if they are a film house).
- [ ] Stress that you (NH Prince) will be the one personally customizing it for them.
